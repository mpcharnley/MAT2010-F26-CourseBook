    Today starts Chapter 3 and our discussion of derivative rules. These are “shortcut” rules, all of which come from the definition of the derivative, to allow us to compute these derivatives more efficiently.

    Linearity of the Derivative.

    Another useful property of the derivative is that it is linear. This means that it plays nicely with addition and constant multiplication.
    🔗

    Example 94.

    Compute the derivative of the function
    \begin{equation*} f(x) = 7x^{20} - 6x^4 + \frac{3}{x^2} + 5e^x\text{.} \end{equation*}
    🔗
